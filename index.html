Where the money goes

Every question you work through is read and coached by a paid AI model, and that is billed to us per question. A long vignette with images costs more than a short one, and turning a whole PDF into an exam costs more again, which is why exam mode sits in its own plan.

  • AI usage. The model that reads the stem, builds the steps and answers when you page the attending.
  • Servers. Sign-in, saved progress and the daily limits.
  • Upkeep. Keeping it working as question banks change their pages.

Prices are set to cover those and keep the tool maintained. The free plan is capped at a few questions a day for the same reason.

Add it to Chrome

Takes about a minute. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc on a computer.

Download the extension
  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called senior-resident.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  4. Open a question. Click the blue Senior Resident tab on the right edge, or press Alt+S.

No extension? No problem

On a phone, tablet or a locked-down computer, use the web app. Paste a question or drop in a screenshot.

Questions

Does it just tell me the answer?

No. It asks you to make each decision and only confirms the answer after you have ruled out everything else yourself. If you want the answer fast, the explanation in your question bank is quicker.

Which question banks does it work with?

Any of them. The extension reads the question on screen on the major banks, and anywhere else you can click its toolbar icon, select the text, or use a screenshot. Senior Resident is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any question bank or exam body.

What happens to my questions?

The question you are on is sent to the AI model to build that session, then kept in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off. It is not sold, shared or used to build a question database. See the privacy page.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. It is an AI study aid and it can make mistakes. When it disagrees with your question bank's answer key, trust the key and check the explanation. It is not medical advice and must not be used for patient care.

Does it change anything in my question bank?

No. It is read only. It never selects an answer, submits a block or edits your notes.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from the Account page. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
