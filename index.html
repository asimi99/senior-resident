Question 14 of 20

A 19-year-old man has 18 hours of abdominal pain that began near the umbilicus and moved to the right lower quadrant. He has nausea and no appetite. Temperature is 38.6°C. There is guarding and rebound at McBurney point.

Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

  1. Mesenteric adenitis
  2. Acute appendicitis
  3. Gastroenteritis
  4. Kidney stone
Senior ResidentStep 1 of 4
Step 1 Key finding

The pain began at the umbilicus, then moved to the right lower quadrant. What does the move mean?

AIt is spreading across the abdomen
BThe lining over one spot is now irritated
CIt is referred from the ureter
CorrectVague midline pain is visceral. Sharp pain in one spot means the lining there is inflamed.

Add it to Chrome

Takes about a minute. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc on a computer.

Download the extension
  1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called senior-resident.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  4. Open a question. Click the blue Senior Resident tab on the right edge, or press Alt+S.

No extension? No problem

On a phone, tablet or a locked-down computer, use the web app. Paste a question or drop in a screenshot.
