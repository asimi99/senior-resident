Questions

Does it just tell me the answer?

No. It never tells you the answer. You make each decision, rule choices out, and one is left standing. Then you check it against your question bank.

Which question banks does it work with?

Any of them. The extension reads the question on screen on the major banks, and anywhere else you can click its toolbar icon, select the text, or use a screenshot. Senior Resident is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any question bank or exam body.

What happens to my questions?

The question you are on is sent to the AI model to build that session, then kept in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off. It is not sold, shared or used to build a question database. See the privacy page.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. It is an AI study aid and it can make mistakes. When it disagrees with your question bank's answer key, trust the key and check the explanation. It is not medical advice and must not be used for patient care.

Does it change anything in my question bank?

No. It is read only. It never selects an answer, submits a block or edits your notes.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from the Account page. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

Something else? Ask in the Help bubble at the bottom right. If it cannot solve it, it goes to a person.
