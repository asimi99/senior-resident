Common questions

Does it just tell me the answer?

No. You make each decision and rule choices out until one is left. Then check it against your question bank.

Which question banks does it work with?

Any of them. My Senior Resident is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any question bank or exam body.

What happens to my questions?

The question you are on is sent to the AI model to build that session, then kept in your own browser. It is not sold, shared or used to build a question database.

Does it work with Anki?

Yes. Install Anki and the free AnkiConnect add-on, and cards go straight into your deck. My Senior Resident is not affiliated with Anki, AnkiWeb or AnKing.

Is it allowed by my question bank?

My Senior Resident is read only. It never clicks, answers or changes anything, never copies screens, and never stores or shares questions. Each bank sets its own rules, so check yours. You are responsible for following them.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. It is an AI study aid and it can make mistakes. When it disagrees with your question bank, trust the bank. It is not medical advice.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, from Manage subscription in your account. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

Add it to Chrome

About a minute. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc on a computer.

Download the extension
  1. Unzip it. You get a folder called senior-resident.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  4. Open a question and click the blue My Senior Resident tab on the right edge, or press Alt+S.

No extension? No problem

On a phone, tablet or locked-down computer, use it right here. Paste a question or drop in a screenshot.
