1. Who is responsible

My Senior Resident is operated from Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. Our privacy officer is accountable for how your personal information is handled. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

3. Question bank content

  • The extension reads the question on your screen only when you press Coach, or when you turn on Auto-read yourself. Auto-read is off by default.
  • It reads only the question on the page you are looking at: the stem, answer choices and images. It does not read other tabs, your history, your question bank account details, scores or anything else.
  • The question is sent once to our AI provider to write your coaching or your Anki card, then discarded. Our server passes it through without saving it and does not log it.
  • We do not build a database of questions, share one user's questions with another, publish them or use them to train any AI model.
  • Cards for Anki go from your browser to Anki on your own computer, through AnkiConnect, or into a file you save. They do not go to our servers except as part of the request that writes them.
  • Images you use for image occlusion stay in your browser and go straight to Anki. If you press Box it for me, the image is sent to our AI provider to place the boxes and is not kept.
  • PDFs you turn into an exam are read in your browser. Their text is sent to our AI provider to turn it into questions and is not kept on our servers. The exam is saved in your browser.

4. The Chrome extension

The extension asks only for the permissions it needs:

  • Access to listed question bank sites, to read the question on screen when you ask. The extension is read only. It never clicks, types or changes anything on those sites.
  • activeTab and scripting, to read the question in the tab you are using when you open the panel.
  • Side panel and offscreen, to show the coach beside your question bank and prepare it in the background when Auto-read is on.
  • Storage, to remember your settings in your browser.
  • Identity, for Sign in with Google.
  • Local address 127.0.0.1:8765, to send cards to AnkiConnect running in Anki on your own computer. This never leaves your computer.

The extension does not take screenshots of your screen.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use. The use of information received from Google APIs and from the Chrome extension will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use data from the extension only to provide the coaching and card features you can see. We do not sell it, transfer it for advertising, use it to decide creditworthiness or lending, or let humans read it, except with your consent, for security, to comply with the law, or when you send it to us in a help request.

5. Why we use it

  • To provide the coaching, cards, exams, history and study plan you ask for.
  • To run your account and subscription and to enforce plan limits and fair use.
  • To answer help requests and fix problems.
  • To keep My Senior Resident secure and prevent abuse, such as trial abuse or shared accounts.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.

We ask your consent when you create an account and accept this policy. We will not use your information for a new purpose without telling you and, where needed, asking again. We will only send marketing email if you opt in, and every such email will have an unsubscribe link, as Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires.

7. Where it is stored

Our providers store and process data outside Canada, mainly in the United States. While it is there it may be accessed by the courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that country.

8. How long we keep it

  • Question text and what you type in the coach: not stored on our servers after the response is written.
  • Account details, history titles and your study plan: while your account is open, and deleted within 30 days after you ask us to delete your account.
  • Help messages and feedback: up to 2 years, so we can follow up and fix recurring problems.
  • Billing records: as long as tax law requires, normally 6 years in Canada.
  • Card fingerprints for the one-trial rule: as long as needed to prevent trial abuse.
  • Data in your browser: until you clear it or delete your sessions.

9. How we protect it

All traffic uses encryption (HTTPS). Database access is limited by row-level security so each account can only read its own data, and only our server functions hold privileged keys. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can use the web app without an account.
  • You can turn Auto-read off, delete sessions, and clear browser data at any time.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, through the Help and feedback form in the app. We will answer within 30 days. Withdrawing consent may mean we cannot provide some features.
  • If you live outside Canada you may have other rights under your local law. Contact us through the Help and feedback form in the app and we will honour them where they apply.

11. Patient information

My Senior Resident is for exam-style questions, not patient care. Do not enter names, health numbers, dates of birth or any other information that could identify a patient. If you do, we will delete it when we find it.

12. Age

My Senior Resident is for adults studying medicine. It is not intended for anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.

14. Contact and complaints

Contact our privacy officer through the Help and feedback form in the app and choose Privacy. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
