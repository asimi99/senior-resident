Senior Resident

Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Senior Resident is a study aid. This page says what it handles and why, in plain language.

What it reads

  • The question you are working on. The extension reads the question on the page you are looking at (the text, the answer choices and any images) only when the panel is open or auto-read is on. It does not read other tabs, your history or anything else on your computer.
  • What you add yourself. Screenshots or text you paste into the app.
  • What you type. Your own reasoning and follow-up questions.

Where it goes

  • The question and your messages are sent to our server and passed to Anthropic's Claude model to write the coaching steps. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models.
  • Your sessions, pearls and statistics are stored in your own browser. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address, your plan and a short title for each question you finish, with Supabase, our database provider.
  • To enforce daily limits we keep a count of requests per account, or per scrambled network address if you are not signed in.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Help and feedback

Questions you type into Help are answered by the same AI provider that coaches your questions. If you send feedback, report an error or pass a help chat to the team, we store what you wrote, your email if you gave one, and basic technical details (app version, browser, the error) so a person can reply and fix it.

What it does not do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No building of a question database from what you study.
  • No changes to your question bank. The extension is read only.

Patient information

Do not enter patient names or other identifying details. Senior Resident is for exam-style questions, not patient care.

Your choices

You can use the app without an account. You can delete your account and its data at any time by asking us through the contact address on the extension's store listing.
