Where the money goes

Every question you work through is read and coached by a paid AI model, and that is billed to us per question. A long vignette with images costs more than a short one, and turning a whole PDF into an exam costs more again, which is why exam mode sits in the top tier.

  • AI usage. The model that reads the stem, builds the steps and answers when you page the attending.
  • Servers. Sign-in, saved progress and the daily limits.
  • Upkeep. Keeping it working as question banks change their pages.

Prices are set to cover those and keep the tool maintained. The free plan is capped at a few questions a day for the same reason.
